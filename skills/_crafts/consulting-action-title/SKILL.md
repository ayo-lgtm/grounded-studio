# craft: consulting-action-title
version: 1.0.0
source: grammar from SlideSpeak consulting packs
used_by: leadership-brief, weekly-ops-review

pattern:
  title: sentence a leader can read in the corridor
  evidence: 1 to 3 bullets or one table snapshot
  footer: block_id or sheet!cell

Bad titles: Overview, Agenda, Key takeaways, Next steps — unless that heading exists in the source.
Do not upgrade "we are considering" into a recommendation.
